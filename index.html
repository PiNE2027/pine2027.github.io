About PiNE

PiNE 2027 — Postdocs and Industry in Nuclear Energy — brings together postdoctoral and equivalent-level researchers working across the UK nuclear energy sector.

Previously known as Postdocs in Nuclear Energy, PiNE has expanded its focus to strengthen collaboration between postdoctoral researchers, academia and industry.

The meeting provides an informal and supportive environment where researchers can share their work, explore collaborative opportunities and learn about developments in nuclear energy research and innovation.

Invited speakers from academia, government and industry will discuss current projects, career opportunities and the future of nuclear energy research in the UK.

Organisers

PiNE 2027 is organised by research staff from the Dalton Nuclear Institute (DNI) at The University of Manchester.

The Dalton Nuclear Institute brings together one of the UK's largest and most diverse academic nuclear research communities, connecting expertise across disciplines to address challenges in nuclear science, engineering and society.
