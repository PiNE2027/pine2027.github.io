Abstract Submission

We welcome abstracts for presentations in the following categories:

  • Materials
  • Robotics, Automation and AI
  • Fuels
  • Radiochemistry
  • Reactors (Fission and Fusion)
  • Waste Management and Disposal
  • Decommissioning and Remediation
  • Regulation and Policy
  • Careers
  • Medicine

Please submit your abstract (maximum 200 words) using the following Microsoft Form: To be released

Please ensure that you use your affiliation email address rather than your personal email address.

Presentation format: Only a limited number of 10-minute oral presentation slots are available. A diverse range of topics will be selected. Depending on the number and range of submissions, some authors may instead be invited to present a poster to share their work with the community.
