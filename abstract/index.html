Abstract Submission

We welcome abstracts for presentations in the following categories:

  • Materials
  • Robotics, Automation and AI
  • Fuels
  • Radiochemistry
  • Reactors (Fission and Fusion)
  • Waste Management and Disposal
  • Decommissioning and Remediation
  • Regulation and Policy
  • Careers
  • Medicine

Please submit your abstract (max 200 words) using the following Microsoft form: To be Released

Please ensure you use your affiliation email address and not your personal email address.

Note: Only a limited number of 10-minute oral presentation are availble with the a diverse range of topics being chosen. Depending on submissions, some authors may be asked to present a poster instead to share their work with the community.
